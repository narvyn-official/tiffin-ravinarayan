Which tiffin plan should I choose?

Our Daily Tiffin at ₹70 per meal is the right pick if you're trying us out, ordering occasionally, or sharing across an office team. You pay only for what you order; no commitments. Each tiffin includes 4 roti, sabzi, dal, rice and salad — a balanced homestyle meal that's filling but not heavy.

The PG Monthly Plan at ₹3500 per month is built for students, PG residents and working professionals who want both lunch and dinner every day with predictable pricing. If you need only lunch or only dinner, the monthly package is ₹1800 per month.

Rice bowls are available day-wise from 9 AM to 9 PM at ₹60. Samosa, Bread and Burger are available on order with a minimum order of 15 pcs; final snack pricing is confirmed on WhatsApp.
